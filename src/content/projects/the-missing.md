[The Missing](https://yuioo.itch.io/the-missing) is a 2D narrative puzzle-horror game. You wake up on the outskirts of Havenswood; your little brother is gone, and you have to find out what happened to Sam.

Built with a team for the UC Berkeley Game Design and Development Decal (Fall 2024). I worked on programming and composed the music.

- **Play:** [yuioo.itch.io/the-missing](https://yuioo.itch.io/the-missing) (HTML5)
